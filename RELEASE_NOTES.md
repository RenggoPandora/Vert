# Vert v1.0.0 — Stop Uploading Your Sensitive PDFs to Random Websites 🎉

Let's be real: we've all uploaded our confidential bank statements, tax forms, or top-secret PowerPoint decks to shady "free online converter" websites just to convert three pages into a Word doc. 

No more. **Vert** is here, and it does all of that straight on your PC — 100% offline, zero internet needed, no file size caps, and absolutely zero paywalls asking for $12/month to split a PDF.

---

### 🚀 What's in the Box?

* **The PDF Swiss Army Knife:**
  * **Visual Page Splitter:** Shows real page thumbnails so you don't have to guess whether page 7 was the invoice or that blank page you accidentally printed in 2021.
  * **Merge PDFs:** Drag, drop, reorder pages, smash together. Done.
  * **Images to PDF:** Dump all your screenshots, photos, and receipts into one neat PDF file.
  * **PDF to Word & Excel:** Converts documents back to editable `.docx` and extracts tables directly into `.xlsx` without screaming.

* **Office Without the Bloat:**
  * `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx` → `.pdf` directly on your desktop.
  * If you have MS Office installed, Vert quietly borrows its engine. If not, it uses our bundled isolated converter. You won't even notice.

* **Image & Media Conversions:**
  * PNG, JPG, WebP, ICO, BMP, TIFF — convert anything to anything.
  * MP4, MKV, MP3, WAV, GIF — powered by a bundled local FFmpeg engine. Yes, you can turn video clips into GIFs offline now.

* **Data Wrangling:**
  * CSV ↔ XLSX ↔ JSON without needing pandas or 2 GB of RAM.

* **Aesthetic & Minimalist:**
  * Styled in **Plus Jakarta Sans** typography.
  * Includes a sleek Dark Mode and Light Mode toggle so your eyes don't melt at 2 AM.
  * Absolutely **zero emojis or AI slop** in the interface. Just crisp vector icons and clean 3D-elevated cards.
  * Ultra-clean boot splash screen by **Hadora Works**.

### 📦 How to Install

1. Grab **`Vert_Setup.exe`** (or the portable `.zip`) from the assets below.
2. Run the installer (it puts a clean shortcut right on your desktop).
3. If Windows 11 Smart App Control shows a block notice:
   * **Right-click `Vert_Setup.exe`** → **Properties** → check **`Unblock`** at the bottom → **OK**.
   * *(Why? Windows flags newly released unsigned open-source binaries until they gain cloud reputation).*
4. Convert files to your heart's content.
